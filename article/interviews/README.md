# Interview Package

**Status:** 11 interviews completed between 16 and 24 November 2026. See `FINDINGS_MEMO.md` for the anonymised findings and `CODING_SUMMARY.csv` for the theme summary.

This folder contains the interview protocol, consent materials, response templates, and coding frame for the RCC geodatabase study.

## Before each interview

1. Read the participant’s department and role.
2. Send the information sheet and consent form.
3. Print two consent forms.
4. Open `RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.md` or `RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.csv`.
5. Prepare a notebook.
6. Confirm the time and place.
7. Test the recorder.
8. Review the questions, but do not read them mechanically.

---

## During the interview

- Start with the opening script in `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.
- Get consent before recording.
- Ask for concrete examples, not opinions only.
- Use the critical-incident prompt for Q07.
- Follow up on surprises.
- Do not ask for personal tax or owner data.
- Do not promise that the system will be built.
- Note documents and systems mentioned.
- Leave time for Q40.

---

## After each interview

1. Complete the response template within 24 hours.
2. Write a short reflection.
3. Assign a participant code (P01, P02, etc.).
4. Store the consent form separately.
5. Log the interview in `INTERVIEW_LOG.csv`.
6. Note any documents promised.
7. Add initial codes in `CODING_FRAME.md`.

---

## How to fill `RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.csv`

Each row is one question. Fill:

- `participant_code` — P01, P02, etc.
- `role_category` — senior, revenue, planning, engineering, waste, GIS-IT, field, partner, public.
- `department` — department or organisation.
- `interview_date` — YYYY-MM-DD.
- `question_id` — Q01–Q40.
- `question` — already filled.
- `response_notes` — a clear summary, not a full transcript.
- `key_quote` — only if you are confident of the wording and have permission.
- `theme` — one or two codes from `CODING_FRAME.md`.
- `follow_up` — anything to clarify or request.
- `quote_permission` — yes / no / conditional.

If you prefer, use one Markdown file per participant (`P01.md`, `P02.md`) based on `RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Note on the article

The interviews are intended to ground the article in RCC’s working environment, not to evaluate individual performance. The strongest material will be:

- concrete workflow narratives;
- examples of address or asset failure;
- descriptions of formal and informal workarounds;
- views on ownership, privacy, and public access;
- priorities and risks for implementation.

These will be used to revise Section 5 (RCC context), Section 6 (proposed system), Section 7 (governance and roadmap), and Section 8 (discussion).
