# Consent Script and Information Sheet

## 1. Information sheet

**Study title:** Making the City Legible: A Human-Centred Geodatabase for Urban Planning and Local Government in Bangladesh

**Researcher:** [Name], [University / affiliation]

**Purpose:** This study examines how Rajshahi City Corporation uses spatial information — maps, addresses, asset records, registers, and drawings — in daily work. It aims to design a municipal geodatabase and governance model that fits the local government working environment.

**What participation involves:** A semi-structured interview of about 45–60 minutes. You will be asked about your role, how you use spatial information, what works, what is difficult, and what a better system would need to do.

**Voluntary participation:** Participation is voluntary. You may decline to answer any question, pause the interview, or withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence for your work.

**Risks and benefits:** There are no direct personal risks. The interview is about work processes, not individual performance. The study may help improve municipal data systems, but there is no guarantee of direct benefit to you.

**Confidentiality:** Your name will not be used in the article. You will be assigned a code such as P05. Identifying details will be removed. Consent forms will be stored separately from interview notes. Data will be stored on a password-protected device and shared only with the research team.

**Recording:** With your permission, the interview will be audio-recorded. If you prefer, the researcher will take notes only.

**Quotes:** If you give permission, short anonymised quotes may be used in the article. You may ask to review or withdraw a quote before publication.

**Data retention:** Data will be kept for [X years] and then securely deleted, in line with university policy.

**Contact:** [Name, email, phone]. If you have concerns, contact [ethics committee / supervisor].

## 3. Verbal consent script

> Before we begin, I want to confirm that you understand the study and agree to take part. You can stop at any time, and you do not have to answer any question you do not want to answer. I will not use your name in the article. May I record this conversation?

If the participant says yes, begin the recording and repeat:

> Thank you. This is interview [code], date [date], with a participant from [role category]. The participant has consented to the interview and to recording.

If the participant says no to recording:

> That is fine. I will take notes only.

---

## 4. Data management note

- Store consent forms separately from interview notes.
- Use participant codes, not names, in transcripts and analysis files.
- Keep a master list linking codes to names in a separate encrypted file, accessible only to the lead researcher.
- Do not send raw audio or identifiable notes by ordinary email.
- Use encrypted storage and secure transfer.
- Remove names of colleagues, individual taxpayers, and complainants from notes.
- If a participant mentions a sensitive case, record only the process, not the person.

---

## 5. Ethics reminder

- Do not interview anyone under 18.
- Do not ask for personal tax, owner, or complaint records.
- Do not ask participants to criticise named colleagues.
- Do not promise that the system will be built or funded.
- If a participant becomes uncomfortable, pause or stop.
- If a disclosure suggests serious wrongdoing, follow the university’s ethics and safeguarding procedure.
