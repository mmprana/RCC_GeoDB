# Document Request List

Ask for **blank, redacted, or public** versions only. Do not request individual tax, owner, complaint, or personnel records.

## Strategic and institutional

- RCC organogram and department responsibilities.
- Annual budget summary and GIS/IT line items.
- City corporation act excerpts relevant to services and planning.
- Digital government or smart city strategy documents.
- Data-sharing agreements or memoranda of understanding.
- IT/digital transformation project documents.
- Training records or capacity assessments (aggregate).

## Spatial data and mapping

- Ward boundary map (paper or digital).
- City boundary map.
- Mouza/JL map, if available.
- Mahalla/neighbourhood map.
- Orthophoto or satellite image metadata.
- List of GIS layers currently held, if any.
- Coordinate system and datum used by engineers.
- Sample CAD drawing (redacted).
- Sample as-built drawing (redacted).

## Property and revenue

- Blank holding-tax assessment form.
- Blank holding register template.
- Holding numbering rules or circulars.
- Property valuation method or manual.
- Sample demand notice (redacted).
- Collection and arrears report template.
- Address or holding correction procedure.

## Engineering and assets

- Road inventory template.
- Drain inventory template.
- Manhole/catchpit inspection form.
- Bridge/culvert inventory form.
- Streetlight inventory or maintenance log.
- Asset condition assessment form.
- Maintenance work-order template.
- Contractor handover checklist.

## Waste and conservancy

- Waste collection route sheet.
- Bin inventory template.
- Collection zone map.
- Vehicle log sheet.
- Complaint register template.
- Transfer station or landfill record template.

## Planning and development

- Zoning map or land-use map.
- Development application form.
- Building permit form.
- Permit register template.
- Plan monitoring or implementation report.
- Heritage list, if public.

## Public services and communication

- Citizen complaint or service request form.
- Ward office service list.
- Public notice template.
- Website or social media analytics (aggregate).
- Public information request log (aggregate).

## Partner agencies

- Data-sharing agreement template.
- List of spatial datasets held by WASA, LGED, RHD, BBS, or SoB.
- Standards or metadata documentation.
- Sample data dictionary.

## What not to request

- Individual taxpayer names, amounts, or arrears.
- Personal contact details.
- Complaint narratives with identifiable information.
- Personnel files.
- Security-sensitive locations or infrastructure details.
- Anything marked confidential without a redaction process.
